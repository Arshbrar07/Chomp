how the loop works

Feed it. Chomp it. Burn it. Repeat.

Four steps, one closed loop. It's not a queue you finish — it's a mouth that never stops moving.

Feed

Deposit any amount into the bot. CHOMP doesn't care how big or how small — everything goes in the same mouth.

what the bot actually does

A utility bot, not just a mascot.

Under the teeth is a plain deposit-and-burn mechanism. Here's what you're actually setting up.

Deposit Anytime

Send tokens to CHOMP whenever you want. No minimum, no maximum, no waiting for a "feeding window."

Pick the Cycle

Choose how often CHOMP chomps — hourly, daily, weekly. The bot bites on schedule, every time, no exceptions.

Auto-Burn

Every bite is sent to a burn address automatically. What's eaten never comes back — you don't have to trust a promise.

Live Supply Tracker

See exactly how much has been fed, chomped, and burned — running totals, no spreadsheets required.

Fraction Mode

Once the pool drops below 1 token, CHOMP switches to eating fractions of fractions. The loop doesn't need to stop to work.

Open Burn Log

Every chomp is a transaction anyone can check. Nothing about the eating happens off the record.
